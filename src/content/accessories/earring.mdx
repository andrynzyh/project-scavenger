Earrings provide bonuses to **Critical Damage**, **Hit Rate**, **Block**, and **Evasion** — ideal for builds focused on accuracy and dodging.

:::tip
**✅** = requires a **Number Change Stone** to reach the maximum value.  
**❌** = already has the maximum value and does not require a Number Change Stone.
:::

:::info
Earrings marked `[Lv.]` can be upgraded through **Digitamamon (Item Make)**. Maximum stat values increase with level.
:::
