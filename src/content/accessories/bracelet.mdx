Bracelets are accessories with their own progression from **Crude Rusty** to the **Spiral Mountain tier**. Higher tiers provide more option slots.

:::tip
**✅** = requires a **Number Change Stone** to reach the maximum value.  
**❌** = already has the maximum value and does not require a Number Change Stone.
:::

:::info
**Ultimate : Yggdrasil Bracelet** is the next upgrade from Royal : Yggdrasil Bracelet — same stats, but no Number Change Stone required.
:::
