Choosing the right class is one of the first important decisions in DMW. Each class has a distinct playstyle, different Digimon requirements, and unique progression paths.

---

## Available Classes

| Class | Role | Difficulty | Best For |
| ----- | ---- | ---------- | -------- |
| **SK DPS** | Skill Damage | ⭐ Easy | Farming, raids, new players |
| **AA DPS** | Auto Attack | ⭐⭐ Medium | Consistent DPS, boss fights |
| **Tank** | Survival | ⭐⭐ Hard | Party support, endgame raids |
| **Support** | Healing/Buff | ⭐⭐⭐ Hard | Party utility, team content |

---

## SK DPS (Skill Damage)

> SK builds focus primarily on dealing damage through Digimon skills.

### Strengths

* High sustained DPS
* Easy to play
* Low DS consumption
* Excellent for long boss fights
* Great for farming

### Weaknesses

* Less burst damage
* Depends heavily on Attack Speed
* Requires strong accessories and seals

### Core Stats Priority

1. Attack (AT)
2. Critical Damage (CD)
3. Attack Speed (AS)
4. Hit Rate (HT)
5. Critical Rate (CT)

### Recommended Journey Digimon

**Dukemon Shin**

### Recommended Seals

| Priority | Stat |
| -------- | ---- |
| 1 | AT |
| 2 | HP |
| 3 | DS |
| 4 | SK |

### Recommended Deck

**Omega**

### Progression Tips

1. Build basic accessories (Underworld → Radiant Holy Beast)
2. Improve seals (aim for 500+ on each stat)
3. Upgrade equipment (Digivice, Goggles)
4. Unlock SSS+ Digimon when ready
5. Optimize Memory Skills

---

## AA DPS (Auto Attack)

> AA builds focus primarily on dealing damage through normal attacks.

### Strengths

* Consistent damage output
* Easier to play than Tank
* Better movement during boss mechanics
* Access to Guardian Memory Skill
* Easier deck progression

### Weaknesses

* Requires more optimized gear
* Needs more offensive stats
* Cannot skip mechanics without very high damage

### Core Stats Priority

1. Hit Rate (HT)
2. Attack (AT)
3. Health Point (HP)
4. Critical Rate (CT)
5. Skill (SK)
6. Digi-Soul (DS)

### Recommended Journey Digimon

**LordKnightmon X**

### Recommended Seals

| Priority | Stat |
| -------- | ---- |
| 1 | HT |
| 2 | AT |
| 3 | HP |
| 4 | CT |

### Recommended Deck

**Main Characters of the Adventure**

### Progression Tips

1. Starter Digimon (Agumon)
2. LordKnightmon X as Journey Digimon
3. Underworld Accessories → Novice Accessories
4. Complete File Island, Glacier, Maze, and Server Continent
5. Build Radiant Holy Beast Gear
6. Royal Base → X-Knight Bracelet
7. D-Ark Lv.1

## Tank

> Tank builds focus on survivability and party protection.

### Strengths

* High survivability
* Essential for endgame raids
* Can survive mechanics that kill DPS
* Party utility through aggro management

### Weaknesses

* Lower personal DPS
* Requires specific gear setup
* Slower solo content clearing
* Less effective in early game

### Core Stats Priority

1. Health Point (HP)
2. Defense (DEF)
3. Attack (AT)
4. Digi-Soul (DS)
5. Resistance

### Recommended Seals

| Priority | Stat |
| -------- | ---- |
| 1 | HP |
| 2 | DEF |
| 3 | AT |
| 4 | DS |

### Progression Tips

1. Focus on HP and DEF seals first
2. Build tank-specific accessories
3. Prioritize survival over damage
4. Coordinate with party for aggro management
5. Invest in resistance stats for endgame

---

## Support (Healing/Buff)

> Support builds focus on party utility through healing, buffs, and debuffs.

### Strengths

* Essential for party content
* High utility through buffs and heals
* Can enable clears that DPS alone cannot
* Unique party role

### Weaknesses

* Low personal DPS
* Dependent on party members
* Complex rotation management
* Not recommended for solo content

### Core Stats Priority

1. Digi-Soul (DS)
2. Healing Power
3. Buff Duration
4. HP
5. Resistance

### Recommended Seals

| Priority | Stat |
| -------- | ---- |
| 1 | DS |
| 2 | HP |
| 3 | Healing |
| 4 | Resistance |

### Progression Tips

1. Master buff/debuff rotations
2. Build DS pool for sustained healing
3. Coordinate with Tank for party utility
4. Focus on buff duration and healing power
5. Invest in survival stats for party content

## Choosing Your Class

### For New Players

**Recommended: SK DPS**

* Easiest to learn
* Good for solo and party content
* Simple rotation
* Fast farming capability

### For Experienced Players

**Consider: AA DPS or Tank**

* AA DPS offers consistent damage with more complexity
* Tank is essential for endgame raid progression
* Both require more gear optimization

### For Party Players

**Consider: Support or Tank**

* Support enables party clears
* Tank is essential for raid survival
* Both are always in demand
