### Intro

The Novice Challenge is a additonal Questline which will guide players through the entire early till endgame, including a lot of nice rewards such as permanent Clothing and titles which will help you on the journey. Due the growing content, the Questline will be updated with more Challeges once every while!

Said Questline can be found in DMW Village (Koromon-Agumon-WarGreymon/Tsunomon-Gabumon-MetalGarurumon, Yamato Npc). During the Questline you will receive a lof of useful item such as Challenge Coin and special Tickets, these can be exchanged at the nearby Taichi Npc in DMW Village. Be aware that you cannot repeat these Questline so choose wiseley.
