Evolution items are required to unlock Digimon evolutions beyond Champion. Each Digimon has a unique evolution path, and the items needed depend on the rank and specific evolution.

---

## Evolution Ranks

| Rank | Unlock Method | Typical Source |
| ---- | ------------- | -------------- |
| **Champion** | Default | — |
| **Ultimate** | Evoluter | Free from packages |
| **Mega** | Evoluter | Free from packages |
| **SS** | SS Evolution Item | Starter/Journey packages |
| **SS+** | SS+ Evolution Item | Quest rewards, dungeon drops |
| **SSS** | SSS Evolution Item | Starter/Journey packages, rare drops |
| **SSS+** | Unique Items | Advanced dungeons, crafting, quests |

---

## Evoluters

Evoluters are the most common evolution unlock item. They are used to unlock Champion → Ultimate and Ultimate → Mega evolutions.

### How to Obtain

* Starter Package (50 Evoluters)
* Journey Package (50 Evoluters)
* Lala's Gift Box (50 Evoluters)
* Dungeon drops
* Event rewards

### Usage

Right-click the Evoluter and select the Digimon to unlock the next evolution tier.

> **Tip:** Save Evoluters for your main Digimon first. Don't waste them on temporary partners.

---

## SS Evolution Items

SS Evolution Items unlock the Super Ultimate (SS) rank evolution for specific Digimon.

### How to Obtain

* Starter Package (SS Evolution Item included)
* Journey Package (SS Evolution Item included)
* Dungeon drops (rare)
* Event rewards

### Usage

Each SS Evolution Item is specific to a Digimon. Use it on the matching Digimon to unlock the SS evolution.

---

## SS+ Evolution Items

SS+ Evolution Items unlock the SS+ rank evolution. These are more powerful than SS but require specific unlock methods.

### How to Obtain

* Questline rewards
* Dungeon boss drops
* Crafting with materials
* Area completion rewards

### Usage

SS+ items are usually quest-locked or dungeon-specific. Follow the unlock guide for your target Digimon.

---

## SSS Evolution Items

SSS Evolution Items unlock the SSS rank — one of the strongest evolution tiers available.

### How to Obtain

* Starter Package (included)
* Journey Package (included)
* Rare dungeon drops
* Event rewards
* NPC exchanges

### Usage

SSS items are Digimon-specific. Use them on the matching Digimon after meeting level and evolution requirements.

---

## SSS+ Evolution Items

SSS+ is the highest evolution rank in DMW. Each SSS+ Digimon has a unique unlock method involving dungeons, quests, crafting, or special materials.

### Common SSS+ Unlock Methods

| Method | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Dungeon Farming** | Clear specific dungeons for materials |
| **Lv.120 Sacrifice** | Sacrifice a Lv.120 Digimon for materials |
| **Crafting** | Combine materials at specific NPCs |
| **Questlines** | Complete multi-step quest chains |
| **X-Antibody Factors** | Alpha and Beta factors combined |
| **Limited Items** | Special event or rare drop items |

### Examples

* **Susanoomon (Shin)** — Requires dungeon farming and crafting
* **Omegamon Alter-B** — Requires Alpha and Beta factors
* **Fanglongmon Shin** — Requires Evil Crystals and Code Crowns
* **Bloomlordmon** — Requires dungeon drops and NPC exchange

> **Tip:** Check each SSS+ Digimon's individual guide for exact requirements.

---

## X-Antibody Factors

Some SSS+ Digimon require X-Antibody Factors (Alpha and Beta) to complete their evolution item.

### How They Work

1. Obtain the **Alpha Factor** from a specific source
2. Obtain the **Beta Factor** from a specific source
3. Combine both at the appropriate NPC
4. The resulting evolution item is **character bound**

### Important Notes

* Alpha and Beta factors are usually from different sources
* The combined item cannot be traded
* Some factors are dungeon-specific
* Check the Digimon guide for exact locations

## Digimon-Specific Items

Many Digimon require unique evolution items that cannot be obtained through standard methods.

### Common Sources

* **Dungeon Bosses** — Rare drops from specific bosses
* **Quest Rewards** — Completion of quest chains
* **NPC Exchanges** — Trade materials for items
* **Event Drops** — Limited-time event rewards
* **Crafting** — Combine materials at NPCs

### Tips

* Always check the Digimon's wiki page for exact requirements
* Some items are **character bound** — cannot be traded
* Some items require specific dungeon clears
* Plan your farming路线 based on your target Digimon

## Progression Path

### Early Game (Starter → SS)

1. Use Starter Package items for SS evolution
2. Use Journey Package items for SSS evolution
3. Save Evoluters for main Digimon

### Mid Game (SS → SSS)

1. Farm dungeons for SS+ items
2. Complete questlines for SS+ rewards
3. Begin SSS+ preparation

### End Game (SSS → SSS+)

1. Follow SSS+ unlock guides
2. Farm dungeon materials
3. Complete crafting requirements
4. Sacrifice Lv.120 Digimon when needed
