# 🎃 Events

## Autumn Equinox

Digimon in the following areas can drop event ingredients:

- Tokyo Area
- Shinjuku Area
- Oil Refinery Area

### Ingredient Drops

| Area | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokyo Area** | Potato, Turkey |
| **Shinjuku Area** | Potato, Egg |
| **Oil Refinery Area** | Potato, Pumpkin |

Ingredients are used for the **Event Exchange** and to craft **Bland Meal**.

Bland Meal can be used to craft:

- **2 of 8 SSS Evolution Items**
- **Dark Power Stone**

Crafting is available through **Joe NPC — Dats Center**.

Joe also offers a Sub Quest that rewards:

**Seasonal Coin [September]**

# 😈 Growing Sin

During the event, players can craft the **Lucemon X Evolution Item** through **Joe NPC — Dats Center**.

### Lucemon X Craft

Required:

- Equinox Meal
- Spiral Mountain Dungeon Cores
- Tokyo Dungeon Materials
- Evil Egg Data
- Orb of Darkness

**Equinox Meal** can be crafted through Joe.

**Orb of Darkness** is obtained by clearing the event-exclusive dungeon:

**Lucemon's Trial**

## Lucemon's Trial

**Solo Event Dungeon**

**Entry:** Birdramon NPC — DMW Village

### Rewards

- Orb of Darkness
- Event Materials

A limited-time quest from **Joe NPC** also rewards a **Cosmetic Nameplate** for clearing the Event Dungeon multiple times.

:::warning
**Lucemon's Trial is Solo.**

The Tamer Skill **Protection cannot be used** inside the dungeon.

## 🌳 Overgrown

### Entrance Ticket

**Overgrown Entrance Tickets** can be claimed every **2 hours**.

**NPC:** Agumon – Bond of Bravery — DMW Village

### How to Claim

1. Press **E**.
2. Open the **Fortune-Time-Event** tab.
3. Claim the ticket.
4. The ticket is delivered through Mail.

:::warning
The **Mail and Overgrown Entrance Ticket expire after 30 minutes**.
:::
