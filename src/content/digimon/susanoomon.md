## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Susanoomon is Jogressed from KaiserGreymon + MagnaGarurumon.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 90+ Tamer
- Lv. 99+ KaiserGreymon
- Lv. 99+ MagnaGarurumon

> 📍 Pick up their quests from **Bokomon** at **DATS Center**.

## KaiserGreymon Quest 🔥

1. Talk to **Bokomon** at DATS Center.
2. Talk to **Angewomon** at Silver Lake.
3. Defeat **Cherubimon** Boss at Silver Lake.
4. Defeat **Dynasmon** Boss at Lost Historic Site.
5. Complete the quest from **Ophanimon** at Silver Lake.

**✅ KaiserGreymon Side Complete**

## MagnaGarurumon Quest 🔵

1. Talk to **Bokomon** at DATS Center.
2. Talk to **Seraphimon** at Distorted Data Village.
3. Defeat **ShadowSeraphimon** Boss at Distorted Data Village.
4. Defeat **LordKnightmon** Boss at Ruined Historic.
5. Complete the quest from **Ophanimon** at Silver Lake.

**✅ MagnaGarurumon Side Complete**

## Important 💡

To Digivolve into Susanoomon, you need:

- 🔹 Jogress Chip
- 🔹 Spirit Accelerator

## Result ✅

**KaiserGreymon + MagnaGarurumon** → ➡️ ⚡ **Susanoomon Unlocked**

# Susanoomon

## Rank

**SSS**

## Requirements


- Lv90+ Tamer
- Lv99 KaiserGreymon
- Lv99 MagnaGarurumon
- Required Spirit Items
- Required Bokomon's Books of Knowledge


## Unlock Guide


### KaiserGreymon

1. Speak with Bokomon in Dats.
2. Continue the Angelwomon progression at Silver Lake.
3. Defeat Cherubimon at Silver Lake.
4. Defeat Dynasmon at Lost Historic Site.
5. Complete the Ophanimon progression.
6. Unlock the KaiserGreymon slot.

### MagnaGarurumon

1. Speak with Bokomon.
2. Continue the Seraphimon progression.
3. Defeat ShadowSeraphimon in Distorted Data Village.
4. Defeat LordKnightmon in Ruined Historic.
5. Complete the Ophanimon progression.
6. Unlock the MagnaGarurumon slot.

### Final

After both lines are unlocked, complete the Susanoomon Jogress
progression.

A Jogress Chip and Spirit Accelerator are required.



## Notes


Spirit Items are obtainable through Susanoomon Raid and Spirit
Summon Cards.

Bokomon's Books of Knowledge are obtainable from the appropriate
Spirit boxes and related sources.
