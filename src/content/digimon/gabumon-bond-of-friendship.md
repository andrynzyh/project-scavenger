## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Gabumon — Bond of Friendship is unlocked from **MetalGarurumon**
via the final **❄️ Last Evolution - Friendship** item.

## Requirements 📋

- Gabumon / MetalGarurumon
- 🔵 Light of Friendship - Alpha
- 🟣 Light of Friendship - Beta
- Final unlock item: **❄️ Last Evolution - Friendship**

## Light of Friendship - Alpha 🔵

1. Enter the **📍 Never-Land Dungeon**.
2. Clear the dungeon and obtain **💎 3× DigiVice of Evolution**.
3. Go to **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft
   **🔵 Light of Friendship - Alpha**.

## Light of Friendship - Beta 🟣

There is no permanent questline for Beta. You need
**🟣 Light of Friendship - Beta**, obtainable from the limited
**📦 Mysterious Crest [Gabumon]** Cash Shop box.

## Final Craft 🔨

Combine at **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**:

- 🔵 Light of Friendship - Alpha
- ➕ 🟣 Light of Friendship - Beta
- ➕ 💰 30T

> 🎁 Receive **❄️ Last Evolution - Friendship**

## Important 💡

- 🔵 **Alpha** = Crafted from DigiVice of Evolution.
- 🟣 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- **DigiVice of Evolution** comes from Never-Land.
- **Light of Friendship - Alpha** and **Last Evolution - Friendship** are
  **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**MetalGarurumon** → ➡️ ❄️ **Gabumon — Bond of Friendship Unlocked**
