## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — RustTyrannomon is Virus / Thunder / Jogress, unlocked from
**Machinedramon + Aegisdramon** (Gizamon + Gomamon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Gizamon

> 📍 Buy **Sampson's Request: RustTyrannomon** from **Calumon** at DATS Center,
> then accept **"Ominous Feeling."**

## Gizamon Quest 🦖

1. Talk to **Richard Sampson → Gallantmon** at DATS Center.
2. Go to **Oil Refinery-3 → Check Oil-Refinery Worker's House → Talk to Craniumon**.
3. Talk to **Kyubimon & Sangloupmon**, then **Gallantmon**.
4. Defeat **Sangloupmon & Kyubimon** until you obtain both **Testimonies**.
5. Follow **Gankoomon's** quests through Oil Refinery-3 → Oil Refinery-1,
   investigate the required locations and talk to **Deputymon & Starmon**.
6. Continue through **Gallantmon → Gankoomon → Craniumon**, then move to
   **Oil Refinery-2** and talk to **Gallantmon**.
7. Defeat **RustTyrannomon** in **Duel Sphere** → Accept DigiVice Quest
   **"Emergency Contact."**
8. Talk to **Akihiro Kurata** at Digimon Maze Entrance.
9. Defeat **RustTyrannomon** in Duel Sphere again → Accept
   **"Reporting the Outcome."**
10. Return to DATS Center → Talk to **Richard Sampson → Nanomon**.

> 🎁 Receive **Tyranno Data: Devouring**.

**✅ Gizamon Side Complete**

## Gomamon Side 🐲

There is no second questline. You need:

- 🔸 **Tyranno Data: Destruction**

Use it to unlock RustTyrannomon for the **Gomamon / Aegisdramon** side. The DMW
guide lists this item as obtainable from a **Cash Loot Box**.

## Important 💡

- You must defeat **RustTyrannomon twice** in Duel Sphere.
- 🦖 **Tyranno Data: Devouring** = Gizamon quest reward.
- 🐲 **Tyranno Data: Destruction** = Gomamon side.
- RustTyrannomon is **Virus / Thunder / Jogress**.

## Result ✅

**Machinedramon + Aegisdramon** → ➡️ 🦖 **RustTyrannomon Unlocked**

# RustTyrannomon

## Rank

**SSS**

## Requirements


- Lv70+ Tamer
- Lv100+ Gizamon


## Unlock Guide


Complete the RustTyrannomon Oil Refinery questline.

### Main progression

1. Obtain Sampson's Request: RustTyrannomon from Calumon.
2. Complete Ominous Feeling.
3. Speak with Richard.
4. Continue the Gallantmon progression.
5. Investigate the Oil Refinery Worker areas.
6. Speak with Craniumon.
7. Investigate the Oil Refinery residents.
8. Collect Sangloupmon's Testimony.
9. Collect Kyubimon's Testimony.
10. Continue the Gankoomon investigation.
11. Investigate Oil Refinery 1.
12. Investigate the DeputyMon / Starmon progression.
13. Complete the RustTyrannomon Duel Sphere encounters.
14. Continue the Kurata progression at Digimon Maze Entrance.
15. Finish the Richard / Nanomon progression.



## Notes


Reward:

**Tyranno Data: Devouring**

The Destruction version is associated with the Gomamon
cash-loot-box source.
