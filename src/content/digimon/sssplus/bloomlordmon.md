## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — BloomLordmon is unlocked from **Palmon [Original]** via the
**Power of Great Nature** craft.

## Requirements 📋

- 40× Palmon [Original]
- Level every Palmon to Lv.120
- 15× Nature Crystal
- 100T

> 📍 Use the **BloomLordmon NPC — DATS Center** for the Palmon sacrifices.

## Great Nature Stones 🌿

1. Level Palmon [Original] → Lv.120.
2. Sacrifice it at the **BloomLordmon NPC — DATS Center**.

> 🎁 Each Lv.120 Palmon gives **1× Great Nature Stone**.

Repeat until you have **🌿 40× Great Nature Stone**.

## Nature Crystals 💎

Clear the **📍 Shopping Mall Dungeon** — defeat the **BloomLordmon Raid** to obtain
**💎 Nature Crystal**.

You need **15× Nature Crystal**.

## Final Craft 🔨

Craft at **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**. Required:

- 40× Great Nature Stone
- 15× Nature Crystal
- 100T

> 🎁 Craft **🌺 Power of Great Nature** — use it to unlock BloomLordmon.

## Important 💡

- You need **40 Palmon [Original] at Lv.120**.
- Sacrificed Palmon are **permanently deleted**.
- **Nature Crystal** comes from the Shopping Mall Dungeon.
- **Power of Great Nature** is **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**Palmon [Original]** → ➡️ 🌺 **BloomLordmon Unlocked**

# BloomLordmon

## Rank

**SSS+**

## Requirements


- Palmon Original x40
- All 40 Palmon must reach Lv120
- Great Nature Stone x40
- Nature Crystal x15


## Unlock Guide


1. Hatch Palmon Original.
2. Level Palmon Original to Lv120.
3. Sacrifice the Lv120 Palmon Original at the BloomLordmon NPC.
4. Repeat until 40 Palmon have been sacrificed.
5. Obtain 40 Great Nature Stone.
6. Obtain Nature Crystal x15 from Shopping Mall Dungeon.
7. Craft **Power of Great Nature**.
8. Use the item to unlock BloomLordmon.



## Notes


Every sacrificed Palmon Original is permanently deleted.

The resulting evolution item is character bound.
