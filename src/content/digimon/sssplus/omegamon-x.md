## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Omegamon X is unlocked from **WarGreymon X + MetalGarurumon X**
and requires **XAI + Jogress Chip**.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 99+ Tamer
- Lv. 99+ Agumon (Classic)
- Omegamon unlocked
- WarGreymon X unlocked

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon \** at **DATS Center**.

## Alpha Quest 🔴

1. Talk to **Magnamon \** at Versandi Terminal.
2. Complete the daily quests until you obtain:
   - 30× Anti-Heterogen
   - 30× X-Evolution Disk
   - 30× Death-X Disk
3. During the questline defeat:
   - **Gallantmon \**
   - 3× Okuwamon
   - 1× Dorugoramon Raid
   - 10× DexDoruGreymon
4. Enter **Royal Base** → talk to **Alphamon Ouryuken** and defeat the **Dexmon
   Raid**.
5. Obtain **30× Yggdrasil's Records**.
6. Talk to **Gallantmon \** → receive:
   🔹 **Omegamon's Chance of Evolution**

## Craft Alpha Factor 🔨

Craft at **📍 PawnChessmon W — D-Terminal**. Required:

- 1× Omegamon's Chance of Evolution
- 20× Anti-Heterogen
- 20× X-Evolution Disk
- 20× Death-X Disk
- 20× Yggdrasil's Records
- 90× Warrior Core
- 5T

> 🎁 **X-Antibody Factor: Omegamon - Alpha**

## Beta Side 🔵

You also need **🔹 X-Antibody Factor: Omegamon - Beta** — there is no Beta
questline. The DMW guide lists Beta as obtainable separately from the
**Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Omegamon X]** Cash Shop box.

## Important 💡

- The quests consume **10 of each** farming material, while crafting needs another
  **20** → that's why you farm **30 total**.
- **Warrior Core** comes from the Omegamon X boss summoned using **Supreme
  Evolution V1 Summon Cards**.
- Omegamon X requires **XAI + Jogress Chip**.

## Result ✅

**WarGreymon X + MetalGarurumon X** → ➡️ ⚔️ **Omegamon X Unlocked**

# Omegamon X

## Rank

**SSS+**

## Requirements


- X-Antibody Factor Omegamon Alpha
- X-Antibody Factor Omegamon Beta


## Unlock Guide


### Alpha

Craft the Alpha factor at PawnChessmon W in D-Terminal.

Requires:

- Anti-Heterogen x20
- X-Evolution Disk x20
- Yggdrasil's Records x20
- Omegamon's Chance of Evolution x1
- Warrior Core x90
- Required currency

### Quest Progression

The progression includes:

1. Gallantmon Jogress Quest.
2. Defeat Okuwamon.
3. Defeat Dorumon.
4. Defeat Dorugamon.
5. Defeat DoruGreymon.
6. Obtain X-Antibody Data Boxes.
7. Convert the required X-Antibody materials.
8. Complete the Magnamon progression.
9. Defeat Dorugoramon.
10. Defeat DexDoruGreymon.
11. Obtain Death-X Data Boxes.
12. Obtain Death-X Disks.
13. Complete the Alphamon Ouryuken progression.
14. Defeat Dexmon.
15. Obtain Yggdrasil's Records.
16. Complete Omegamon's Chance of Evolution progression.

### Beta

Obtain:

**Mysterious X-Antibody Factor Omegamon**

from the limited source.

Combine Alpha and Beta.



## Notes


The questline consumes some of the required materials, while the
final crafting stage requires additional copies.

Yggdrasil's Records have a special channel-change interaction in
the original guide.
