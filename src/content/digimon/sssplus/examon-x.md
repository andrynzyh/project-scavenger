## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Examon X is unlocked from **Slayerdramon X + Breakdramon X** and
requires **XAI + Jogress Chip** to Digivolve.

## Requirements 📋

- Examon unlocked
- X-Antibody Factor Examon Alpha
- X-Antibody Factor Examon Beta

## Alpha Factor 🔴

1. Obtain **Supreme Evolution V2 Summon Cards** from:
   - Colosseum Hero R10–R15
   - Colosseum Extreme R1–R6
2. Use the cards to summon and defeat the **Examon X Boss**.
3. Farm **🔸 200× Emperor's Will**.
4. Take these materials to **UlforceVeedramon FM at DATS Center**:
   - 200× Emperor's Will
   - 10× Fanronkou
   - 25T

> 🎁 Craft **X-Antibody Factor Examon Alpha**.

## Beta Factor 🔵

There is no Beta questline. You need
**🔹 X-Antibody Factor Examon Beta** — obtainable from the limited
**Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Examon X]** Cash Shop box.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Alpha** = Crafted.
- 🔵 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- Alpha is **Character Bound**.
- Examon X requires **XAI + Jogress Chip** to Digivolve.

**Slayerdramon X + Breakdramon X** → ➡️ 🐉 **Examon X Unlocked**

# Examon X

## Rank

**SSS+**

## Requirements


- X-Antibody Factor Examon X Alpha

## Unlock Guide


### Alpha

Craft the Alpha factor at the UlforceVeedramon Future Mode NPC.

Requires:

- Emperor's Will x200
- Fanronkou x10
- Required currency

### Emperor's Will

Obtain Emperor's Will from the Examon X boss.

The boss is summoned with:

**Supreme Evolution V2 Summon Card**

The cards are obtained from:

- Colo Hero Rounds 10–15
- Extreme Rounds 1–6 reward boxes

### Beta

Obtain:

**Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Examon X]**

from the limited source.

Combine Alpha and Beta.
