## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Ordinemon is unlocked from
**Raguelmon + Ophanimon Falldown Mode** (Meicoomon + Salamon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Meicoomon
- Sampson's Request: Ordinemon

> 📍 Buy **Sampson's Request: Ordinemon** from **Calumon** at DATS Center,
> then accept **"Recall Order."**

## Meicoomon Quest 😈

1. Talk to **Richard Sampson** at DATS Center → then **Miki** at Big Sight.
2. Enter **Duel Sphere** and defeat **ImperialDramon**.
3. Talk to **Nanomon → Richard Sampson**, then **Thomas** at Shinjuku Easter
   Area (Night).
4. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat **Meicrackmon Vicious Mode**.
5. Continue through **Akihiro Kurata → Thomas → Richard Sampson → Thomas** at
   Odaiba.
6. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat:
   - Raguelmon
   - MirageGaogamon
7. Go to **Minato City** and continue through **Ophanimon → Akihiro Kurata**.
8. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat **Raguelmon**.
9. Talk to **Digimon Kaiser → Raguelmon** at Minato City.
10. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat **Ordinemon**.
11. Talk to **Digimon Kaiser → Ophanimon** and accept the DigiVice Quest
    **"Return."**

> 🎁 Receive **Data of Fallen Angel - Wrath**.

**✅ Meicoomon Side Complete**

## Salamon Side 😇

There is no additional questline. You need:

- 🔹 **Data of Fallen Angel - Sorrow**

Use it to unlock Ordinemon for **Salamon**. The DMW guide lists it as obtainable
from a **Cash Loot Box**.

## Important 💡

- 😈 **Wrath** = Meicoomon quest reward.
- 😇 **Sorrow** = Salamon side.
- You will enter Duel Sphere **multiple times**, including the final Ordinemon
  battle.
- Don't forget to accept **"Return"** after the final fight.

## Result ✅

**Raguelmon + Ophanimon Falldown Mode** → ➡️ 😈 **Ordinemon Unlocked**

4. Complete the Imperialdramon Duel Sphere encounter.
5. Continue the Nanomon / Richard progression.
6. Complete the Thomas / Shinjuku Easter Night progression.
7. Complete the Meicrackmon VM Duel Sphere encounter.

9. Complete the Odaiba progression.
10. Complete the Raguelmon + MirageGaogamon Duel Sphere encounter.
11. Continue the Richard / Ophanimon progression.
12. Complete the Raguelmon encounter.
13. Complete the Digimon Kaiser progression.
14. Defeat Ordinemon in Duel Sphere.
15. Finish the final Ophanimon / DigiVice return progression.
