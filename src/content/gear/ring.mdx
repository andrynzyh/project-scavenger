Rings are the primary accessory, providing various stat bonuses. The higher the tier, the more **Option slots** and the greater the maximum stat values.

:::tip
**✅** = requires **Number Change Stone** to reach the maximum value.  
**❌** = comes with the maximum value already; no Number Change Stone required.
:::

- **Ring of the Marine Dragon** — all stats appear twice in a single combination.
:::
