Necklaces provide unique stat bonuses, including **Critical Damage** and **Attack Speed**, which are not available on Rings.

:::tip
**✅** = requires **Number Change Stone** to reach the maximum value.  
**❌** = comes with the maximum value already; no Number Change Stone required.
:::

---

## Recommended Necklaces

| Name | Options | NCS | How to Obtain |
|------|:-------:|:---:|------------|
| Xuanwumon Sealed Shiny Necklace | 4 | ❌ | Xuanwumon (Raid) Box |
| Flame Necklace of Sealed Zhuqiaomon | 4 | ❌ | Zhuqiaomon (Raid) Box |
| Aural Necklace of Sealed Baihumon | 4 | ❌ | Blossomon (Raid) Box / Baihumon (Raid) Box |
| Qinglongmon's Sealed Radiant Necklace | 4 | ❌ | Qinglongmon (Raid) Box |
| Miracle Necklace (Special) | 5 | ✅ | Trade Miracle Necklace [Lv.10] + Qinglongmon's Sealed Radiant Necklace — Magnamon NPC |
| Sealed Necklace of Four Holy Beasts [Lv.1–5] | 4 | ✅ | Digitamamon (Item Make) — materials from Uprising Flame (Hard) |
| Sealed Radiant Necklace of Four Holy Beasts [Lv.1–5] | 4 | ✅ | Digitamamon (Item Make) — materials from Descending Thunder God (Hard) |
| Fanglongmon's Ancient Necklace [Lv.1–10] | 4 | ✅ | Fanglongmon (Raid) / Digitamamon (Item Make) |
| Necklace of the Wooden Puppet [AA/SK/TA] | 5 | ✅ | Taichi (Spiral Mountain) — materials from Front Yard of Marionette Mansion |

- **Divine Radiant Necklace of Four Holy Beasts Lv.5** is the next upgrade from the Radiant Necklace Lv.5 — same values, but no Number Change Stone required.
- **Fanglongmon's Ultimate Ancient Necklace [Lv.10]** is the next upgrade from the Ancient Necklace Lv.10 — same values, but no Number Change Stone required.
:::
