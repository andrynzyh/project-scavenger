Corrupted Accessories are the highest accessory tier from **Spiral Mountain**. They are crafted from Taichi (Spiral Mountain) at the DATS Center.

- **Options** can be rerolled with **Option Change Stone**
- **Renewal** can be increased with **Renewal Increase Stone**
- **Digitary Power** can be increased with **Dark Power Stone** or **Void Power Stone**
  - Dark Power Stone: dropped by Spiral Mountain dungeon bosses
  - Void Power Stone: crafted at the Taichi NPC (DATS Center) or dropped from a Void Space Box

:::info
Corrupted Accessories start at **100% Digitary Power** and can be increased to **130%**.  
**❌** = no Number Change Stone required — already has the maximum value.
:::
